The evaporating temperature is the lower of two limits, a minimum approach
at the hot end of the evaporator,
$T_\mathrm{3e}=\overline{T}_\mathrm{2d}-\Delta T_\mathrm{2D,3E}$, and the
largest boiling temperature for which the composite curves retain
$\Delta T_\mathrm{pinch,ORC}$ everywhere; for the conditions considered here
the pinch is the binding constraint in every case.
